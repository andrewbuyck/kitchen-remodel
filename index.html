Back Porch: A Covered Seat by the Doors

Somewhere to sit just outside the dining room's French doors, under a roof. Each cover is shown over two porches, one hardwood deck and one raised bluestone. Both sit one step (about 7½ in) below the doors: building code allows that at a door that isn't the house's main exit, and it buys headroom under every cover without touching the roof. A new roof can't sit higher than the existing eave because the upstairs windows are just above it, so the flat roof and the gable keep their eaves at the old roof line; with the step down and a beam flush with the ceiling, the flat roof gets about 8 ft of headroom. It's shown 11 ft deep over the full porch and 8 ft deep over a porch shortened to match. The gable can also open into its pediment with a curved arch (about 9 ft 5 in at the centre). The gable hips back into the old roof and stops short of the solar panels, so none of them need to move. The two aluminium pergolas are off-the-shelf kits. Each cover also brings string lights out to two cedar posts on the lawn. In the evening views the porch and string lights are on (they run dusk to dawn).
